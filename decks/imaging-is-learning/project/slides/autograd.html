The method

From hand-derived updates to autograd

Classic · Gerchberg–Saxton, ePIE

Alternating projections

Force each prediction to match its measured amplitude, then update the part of the spectrum that produced it.

Change the physics, and you derive a new update rule.

ImAGeD

A differentiable forward model

Write the simulation of the microscope once. Autograd supplies the gradients.

Change the physics, and nothing else changes.

Not new in itself: FP has been written in TensorFlow (Jiang et al., 2018), and the classic algorithms are effectively gradient descent (Zuo et al., 2016).
